What we make

  • Football, rugby and basketball jerseys
  • Cricket whites and coloured kits
  • Match and training shorts
  • Tracksuits and warm-up jackets
  • Training tops and quarter-zips
  • Esports and fan jerseys

Fabrics

  • Interlock and bird-eye mesh polyester (130–180 GSM)
  • Recycled polyester options
  • Moisture-wicking knitted pique
  • Woven microfiber for tracksuits

Branding & decoration

  • Full sublimation (all-over print, no colour limit)
  • Heat-transfer names and numbers
  • Embroidered and silicone crests
  • Tackle twill

Order details

Minimum order is 25 pieces per design, with sizes mixed freely. We start with a pre-production sample, then move to bulk once you approve it. You get photo updates during production and can request a live video call from the factory at any stage.

Shipping to Germany, the UK, the USA, France and Australia by express courier, air freight or sea freight.

FAQ

Common questions

Can every player have their own name and number?

Yes. Send us a roster spreadsheet with sizes, names and numbers and we personalise each shirt at no extra setup cost.

How many colours can a sublimated kit have?

Sublimation prints the whole garment, so there is no colour limit and no extra cost for gradients or photo-style designs.

Do you make kits for youth teams?

Yes. We grade youth sizes from 4 years upward and can mix adult and youth sizes in one order.

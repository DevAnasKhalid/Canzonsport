About this business

Canzon Sport is a custom clothing and sportswear manufacturer in Sialkot, Pakistan. OEM & private label: hoodies, streetwear, gym wear, team kits, boxing & MMA gear, martial arts uniforms, jackets, leather wear, caps and patches. Low MOQ from 25 pieces per design, samples in 7–12 days, bulk in 20–35 days. Custom logos, embroidery, printing and sublimation. Shipping to the USA, UK, Europe, Canada and Australia.

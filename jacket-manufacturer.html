Order details

Minimum order is 25 pieces per design, with sizes mixed freely. We start with a pre-production sample, then move to bulk once you approve it. You get photo updates during production and can request a live video call from the factory at any stage.

Shipping to Germany, the UK, the USA, France and Australia by express courier, air freight or sea freight.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you make real leather varsity sleeves?

Yes. We offer genuine cowhide leather and PU leather sleeves; Sialkot has a long leather-working tradition.

What filling do your puffer jackets use?

Polyester wadding is our standard. Recycled fill and down-alternative fills are available on request.

What is the MOQ for jackets?

25 pieces per design. Complex jackets with many patches take a little longer to sample.

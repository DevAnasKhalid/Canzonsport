What we make

  • High-waist leggings and bike shorts
  • Sports bras (low, medium and high support)
  • Tank tops, stringers and crop tops
  • Oversized pump covers and lifting tees
  • Training shorts with liners
  • Matching two-piece sets

Fabrics

  • Nylon-spandex 75/25 (230–280 GSM)
  • Polyester-spandex brushed jersey
  • Cotton-spandex for lifestyle pieces
  • Recycled nylon on request

Branding & decoration

  • Silicone and reflective heat transfers
  • 3D embossing and debossing
  • Tonal embroidery
  • Woven and printed neck labels

Order details

Minimum order is 25 pieces per design, with sizes mixed freely. We start with a pre-production sample, then move to bulk once you approve it. You get photo updates during production and can request a live video call from the factory at any stage.

Shipping to Germany, the UK, the USA, France and Australia by express courier, air freight or sea freight.

FAQ

Common questions

Are your leggings squat-proof?

We test every new fabric with a stretch and opacity check before sampling, and we send swatches so you can check them yourself.

Can you match my brand's Pantone colours?

Yes. Nylon-spandex is dyed to order once you reach the dyeing minimum; below that we offer stock colours.

Do you make seamless activewear?

Our core range is cut-and-sew. Seamless knitting is available for selected styles; ask us for current MOQs.

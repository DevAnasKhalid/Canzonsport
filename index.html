What we make

Six product lines, one factory

Every piece is made to your spec: fabric, fit, colours, trims, labels and packaging.

Sportswear & Team Kits

Match kits, training wear and tracksuits for football, cricket, basketball, rugby and esports teams, made in our Sialkot factory to your colors and crest.

  • Full sublimation
  • Heat-transfer names and numbers
  • Embroidered and silicone crests

Gym & Fitness Wear

Leggings, sports bras, tanks, joggers and lifting tees for fitness brands, gyms and personal trainers. Squat-proof fabrics, flatlock seams and your own fit.

  • Silicone and reflective heat transfers
  • 3D embossing and debossing
  • Tonal embroidery

Streetwear & Casual

Heavyweight hoodies, boxy tees, joggers and sets with the details streetwear customers notice: dropped shoulders, ribbed cuffs, garment dye and clean labels.

  • Screen printing and puff print
  • DTG and DTF printing
  • Flat and 3D embroidery, chenille

Jackets & Outerwear

Varsity, bomber, puffer, softshell and windbreaker jackets with custom linings, patches and hardware, built for brands, teams and schools.

  • Chenille and felt patches
  • Embroidered back panels
  • Custom zip pullers and snaps

Martial Arts & MMA

BJJ gis, karate and taekwondo uniforms, rash guards, fight shorts and boxing gloves for academies, clubs and fight brands.

  • Embroidered chest and shoulder patches
  • Sublimated rash guards
  • Woven lapel labels

Uniforms & Workwear

Polo shirts, staff uniforms, chef wear and workwear that hold their shape through industrial washing, with your logo embroidered or printed.

  • Logo embroidery
  • Screen printing and DTF
  • Reflective tape

How it works

From idea to delivery in five steps

One account manager stays with your order the whole way, with photo and video updates at every stage.

Step 1

Brief & quote

Share a design, tech pack or reference photo. We confirm fabric, sizes and price.

24–48 hrs
Step 2

Sampling

We make a pre-production sample and send it by express courier for approval.

7–12 days
Step 3

Bulk production

Cutting, printing or embroidery and stitching begin once you approve the sample.

20–35 days
Step 4

Quality check

Pieces are measured and inspected against the approved sample before packing.

Every order
Step 5

Shipping

Packed and labelled to your spec, then sent by express air, air freight or sea.

3–7 days by air

Custom t-shirt manufacturing

Canzon Sport is a custom t-shirt manufacturer in Sialkot, Pakistan. These styles are reference designs that show what our factory makes: heavyweight, oversized, boxy and classic tees with screen printing, puff print, DTG, DTF and embroidery. We do not sell these exact designs. We make your own version with your artwork, fabric, colours and labels from 25 pieces per design.
